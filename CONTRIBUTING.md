# Contribution and reporting policy

Jira Description Autosave is a maintainer-developed utility shared publicly for others to use, inspect, and fork under the MIT license. It is not accepting external contributions. Pull requests are disabled; please do not open an issue to submit a patch or request an exception.

## Bug reports

First-hand, reproducible bug reports are welcome through the issue form. Search existing issues and check the README's scope and limitations first. Include the extension and browser versions, the Jira view involved, clear steps, and what you expected versus what happened. Small, concrete reports are most useful.

Use a disposable issue when reproducing problems. Remove customer information, Jira descriptions, credentials, and private URLs from any screenshots or logs. You do not need to disclose your Jira site or issue key.

AI-assisted wording is fine, but you must personally verify every claim and reproduction step. Do not submit unverified generated reports, bulk scanner output, speculative audits, or generic improvement lists. Issues are for bugs you encountered, not marketing, offers of services, or contribution requests.

Reports that do not meet these requirements may be closed without discussion. Repeated spam or abuse may result in blocking and reporting the account. There is no guaranteed response time or commitment to implement a request.
